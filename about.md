I'm a Senior AI Engineer based in Toronto, focused on LLM systems and ML platform engineering. Currently at EvenUp, where I architected the LLM-as-judge evaluation platform that scores generated legal documents across writing quality, factual correctness, and formatting — evaluating 600–800 documents per week and feeding subject-matter-expert annotations back into the eval framework and the underlying document-generation quality.

Before EvenUp, I spent the better part of a decade building ML platforms and data infrastructure at scale: MLegobricks at Ripple (a unified ML monorepo adopted by 12 teams), Flyte rollout at Arctic Wolf (the company's first AWS EKS Flyte deployment), and Anahit at Borealis AI (a config-driven ML data platform processing 30 TB of data per day).

My interests sit at the intersection of distributed systems, ML infrastructure, and applied AI. I enjoy building robust, scalable systems that make ML and LLM workflows reliable in production.

## Technical Focus

- LLM evaluation systems and applied AI engineering
- ML platform engineering and infrastructure
- Production model serving and deployment
- Distributed data processing at TB-scale
